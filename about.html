Katse Civils Mining and Project Management combines practical site knowledge, civil construction expertise, and disciplined project delivery to support infrastructure that stands the test of time.

We work across the public and private sectors to support infrastructure works that require careful planning, technical understanding, and safe execution. Our work is grounded in practical field experience and a clear focus on quality, safety, and long-term performance.

From inspections and rehabilitation to bridge works and civil project coordination, we help clients move from concept to completion with confidence.
