About

Leadership built on infrastructure experience

Katse Civils Mining and Project Management is a Pretoria-based civil and structural engineering company delivering competent, people-focused infrastructure solutions for construction and rehabilitation works.

We support clients across the public and private sectors with dependable execution, technical oversight, and practical project management. Our work is grounded in sound engineering principles, safe delivery, and a commitment to quality throughout the project lifecycle.

From site inspections and assessments to bridge works and civil infrastructure upgrades, we bring disciplined leadership and measurable value to every assignment.
